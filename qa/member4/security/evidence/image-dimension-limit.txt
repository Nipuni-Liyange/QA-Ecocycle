SEC-17 – Image Dimension Validation

**Test input:** 5,000 × 5,000 pixel JPEG (25 million pixels), below 2 MB.

**Expected result:** The application rejects images exceeding 24 million pixels.

**Actual result:** The application displayed the validation message:

"This photo could not be processed. Use an undamaged JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 2 MB and 24 megapixels."

**HTTP/UI result:** Image rejected; pickup was not submitted.

**Status:** PASS

**Conclusion:** The application enforces the maximum image dimension limit.
